Background & Education

Dr. Idan Y. Lavian earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from Touro College of Dental Medicine. His foundation in comprehensive oral health is built on a passion for helping patients achieve confident, healthy smiles.


Following his doctoral studies, he completed a rigorous General Practice Residency at Montefiore Medical Center, mastering complex cases in high-stakes hospital settings.

Expertise

Dr. Lavian specialized further by working under the guidance of leading Endodontists, refining his precision in root canal therapy and advanced endodontic procedures.


Today, he integrates evidence-based dentistry with a commitment to lifelong learning, ensuring every patient benefits from the latest dental innovations.
